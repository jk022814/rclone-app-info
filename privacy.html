Privacy policy

Application: John Homelab rclone
Effective date: October 25, 2026

Purpose and Google Drive access

This Google OAuth client is for the owner's personal rclone backup workflow. Access to Google Drive is limited to the authorization the owner grants. rclone uses that access to transfer and inspect backup files and associated metadata, such as file names, identifiers, sizes, and checksums where available.

Storage and use of data

Backup contents and related metadata are handled on owner-controlled systems and in the owner's Google Drive. OAuth credentials are handled by rclone on those systems; this static information website does not receive or store them. Backup data and credentials are not intended for publication in this site's public repository.

The application is not an advertising, data-sale, or public analytics service. Its purpose is backup transfer, verification, and management. Google and any hosting or storage providers separately process data under their own applicable terms and privacy policies.

Retention, revocation, and deletion

Backup retention is controlled by the owner. Removing application access does not automatically delete existing backup files. The owner can review or revoke the application's Google access in Google Account third-party connections, and can separately manage stored backup files and local rclone credentials.

This website

These static pages contain no sign-in form, advertising code, or custom analytics. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process visitor information as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact

For non-sensitive questions, use the public project issue tracker. Do not post passwords, OAuth credentials, backup contents, or other private information.
